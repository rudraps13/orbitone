ORBITONE - HOW TO PLAY
======================

Double-click the "Orbitone" icon on your desktop.
Click "Start orbiting".


THE IDEA
--------
You are a little spark of light, circling a planet.
The planets above you are asleep (grey).
Fly to them to wake them up. Each one bursts into colour and plays
a musical note, so the further you go, the longer your song.


CONTROL - JUST ONE
------------------
TAP (mouse click, touch, or SPACE bar) to let go of the planet.

- While you circle, a DOTTED LINE shows the way you'll fly.
- Wait until the dotted line points at the next planet.
- TAP. You fly in a straight line.
- Reach the dotted ring around the next planet and you start
  circling it.


SCORE
-----
+1           for each planet you wake up
PERFECT      fly straight in without the planet pulling you sideways.
             Your combo goes up (x2, x3 ... up to x8) and you get
             bonus points. Missing a perfect resets the combo to x1.
+1           for each gold stardust diamond you fly through


GAME OVER
---------
If you miss and fly off into space, the game ends.
Your best score is saved.


TIPS
----
- Don't rush. The spark keeps circling, so you can wait for the
  perfect moment.
- Planets gently PULL you in, so you don't have to be exact.
- Later planets are smaller, further apart, and some MOVE side to
  side. Watch them before you tap.
- Turn the sound ON. Every planet plays a note, and it sounds
  better the longer you last.
- The "Sound on / Sound off" button is at the top right.
